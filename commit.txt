Extract DAG engine into @podpiper/dag workspace package and restructure project

Split the generic DAG execution engine (graph, cache, exec-state, types,
define-action) into a standalone Bun workspace package under packages/dag/.
It has no dependencies on the rest of the codebase.

Other changes included in this cleanup:

- Restructure CLI into per-command directories (sync/, check/, graph/) with
  colocated helpers (render.ts, mermaid.ts)
- Colocate rss/ and paths.ts into pipeline/ next to their consumers
- Consolidate NodeKind and defineActionWithPorts into actions/define-action
- Compute node hashes at insertion time instead of execution time
- Replace ProgressCallback with structured ExecAction emissions
- Combine original video description with AI summary in episode descriptions
